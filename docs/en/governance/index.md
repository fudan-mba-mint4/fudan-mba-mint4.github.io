# Class Committee

## Governance Philosophy

> **The committee is not a management body, but an ecosystem enabler — every student can be seen, needed, and remembered.**

We believe that an excellent class is not "managed" by a few people, but built through an empowerment mechanism that allows everyone to participate, contribute, and grow. The committee's role is to build platforms, reduce collaboration costs, and unlock individual energy — not to issue top-down directives.

## Six Functional Modules

Class governance consists of six functional modules covering learning, activities, finance, memory, research, and overall coordination.

### Class Lead (1 person)

- **Liaison with the school**: Serves as the official contact between the class and the school, conveying requirements and feeding back class needs
- **Overall coordination**: Sets the class development direction and coordinates the pace of all modules
- **Cross-group resource matching**: Connects different modules and students to facilitate resource flow
- **Information filtering & semester theme**: Filters redundant information and sets the core atmosphere and theme for each semester

### Deputy Leads (2 persons)

- **Daily affairs & progress tracking**: Handles daily class operations and tracks execution progress
- **Committee organization**: Organizes regular committee meetings to ensure information sync and timely issue resolution
- **Task calendar & follow-up**: Maintains the class task calendar to ensure important items are not missed and are closed out

### Experience Officers (2+ persons)

- **Activity incubation & lifestyle intel**: Plans and organizes class activities, collects and shares lifestyle information
- **Class atmosphere maintenance**: Monitors the overall class mood and atmosphere, adjusts and guides as needed
- **Everyone is seen · Encourage quieter students**: Pays special attention to less vocal students, creating opportunities for everyone to be seen and heard

### Finance & Incentive Officers (2+ persons)

- **Transparent class fund & ledger disclosure**: Manages class fund income and expenses, regularly publishes the ledger
- **Class coin system operation**: Designs and operates the class internal incentive points system
- **Budget mechanism & small incentive items**: Establishes the class budget mechanism and purchases small incentive items to recognize contributions

### Memory Curators (2+ persons)

- **Visual recording & content output**: Captures classroom, activity, and daily moments; produces photos and videos
- **Class brand building**: Shapes and promotes the class brand image, enhancing recognition and cohesion
- **Material production & class culture integration**: Designs and produces class merchandise incorporating cultural elements

### Knowledge Researchers (2+ persons)

- **Course breakdown & knowledge base**: Organizes and breaks down course content, builds and maintains the class knowledge base
- **AI tools & learning empowerment**: Explores and promotes AI tools to improve the whole class's learning efficiency
- **Learning resources & demand-driven supply**: Precisely supplies learning resources and methods based on actual student needs
