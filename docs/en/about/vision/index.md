# Class Vision

## Vision Statement

> **Build a class ecosystem where every student can be seen, needed, and remembered — growing together during the two-year MBA journey, and becoming each other's lifelong resources and partners after graduation.**

## Core Philosophy

### The Committee Is Not a Manager, but an Ecosystem Enabler

We reject the traditional "committee manages students" model, and instead build an **empowerment-oriented governance system**. The committee's responsibilities are not to issue orders, but to:

- Build platforms where everyone can participate
- Reduce collaboration costs between students
- Unlock individual strengths and passions
- Make contributions visible and efforts recognized

In this system, there is no divide between "managers" and "the managed" — only collaborators in different roles.

### Every Student Can Be Seen, Needed, and Remembered

This is the core of our class vision and the measure of all our work:

- **Seen**: Everyone's presence, contributions, and bright spots can be perceived and recognized by the class
- **Needed**: Everyone's abilities, experience, and resources can find a place to be used in the class
- **Remembered**: Everyone's stories, moments, and friendships can be recorded and cherished

## Expectations for the Two-Year MBA Journey

### Year 1: Rooting & Connecting

- Build deep friendships and form class cohesion
- Lay a solid foundation in management studies and build a systematic knowledge framework
- Explore personal career directions and clarify growth paths
- Form a consensus on class culture and establish governance mechanisms

### Year 2: Deepening & Blooming

- Apply knowledge to practice, completing the transformation from theory to action
- Deepen professional expertise and form differentiated competitive advantages
- Expand professional networks and connect with broader resources
- Preserve class memories and prepare for graduation

## Post-Graduation Expectations (Class of 2026)

### Lifelong Learning Community

Graduation is not the end, but a new beginning. We expect the class to become a **lifelong learning community**:

- Continuously share industry insights and career experience
- Regularly organize reunions and exchange activities
- Maintain the class knowledge base and continuously accumulate collective wisdom

### Each Other's Resources & Partners

- Recommend and support each other in career development
- Seek cooperation opportunities in entrepreneurship and projects
- Witness and bless each other at important life moments

### Brand Legacy of Fudan MBA Mint 4

- Participate in alumni activities under the class name
- Provide experience and guidance to subsequent classes
- Make "Mint 4" a warm and influential collective symbol
