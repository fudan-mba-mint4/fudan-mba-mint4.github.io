# Class Culture

## Class Slogan

<div style="text-align:center; padding: 2rem 0; font-size: 2rem; font-weight: 700; color: var(--vp-c-brand-1);">

4 the Best, for the Future.

</div>

This slogan cleverly embeds "Class 4" while expressing our shared belief in pursuing excellence and facing the future.

## Core Values

### Learning & Growth

Maintain an open mind for continuous learning, constantly breaking cognitive boundaries during the MBA journey, and transforming classroom knowledge into practical ability.

### Resource Sharing

Everyone is both a contributor and a beneficiary of resources. We advocate information exchange, experience sharing, and opportunity co-creation, letting collective wisdom amplify individual value.

### Unity & Mutual Support

Treat each other sincerely and support one another. Whether facing academic pressure or life challenges, we are each other's most reliable partners.

### Pursuit of Excellence

Never settle for "good enough" — strive for higher standards in everything. Excellence is not a destination, but our shared way of life.

## Class Emblem Meaning

The class emblem is a visual symbol of our identity, with each element carrying specific meaning:

| Element | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **Circular badge** | Symbolizes unity and completeness, representing the class as a closely connected whole |
| **Sailboat pattern** | Signifies setting sail and braving the waves, symbolizing the exploratory spirit of the MBA journey |
| **Mint green theme color** | Represents freshness, vitality, and growth, echoing the "Mint" class name |
| **"FUDAN UNIVERSITY"** | Identifies our institution, reflecting Fudan identity |
| **"MBA 2026"** | Marks our graduating class, recording our two-year shared journey |

## Origin of the Class Name

### Mint 4

"Mint" symbolizes freshness, vitality, and growth — just like us, a group of students returning to campus at a critical stage of our careers, with a thirst for new knowledge and expectations for the future, setting out again in the Fudan MBA classroom.

Mint green is our theme color — neither too loud nor lacking in vitality, just like our class temperament: low-key but powerful, gentle but purposeful.

"4" is not only our class number but also forms a pun with the slogan "4 the Best," signifying our pursuit of the best self, the best class, and the best future.

## Cultural Atmosphere

We are committed to fostering a **sincere, open, warm, and upward** class atmosphere:

- **Sincere**: No pretense, no posturing — interact as our true selves
- **Open**: Embrace diverse backgrounds and viewpoints, encourage pluralistic expression
- **Warm**: Care for each other, support one another, so everyone feels a sense of belonging
- **Upward**: Motivate each other, progress together, never leave anyone behind

Here, everyone can **be seen, be needed, and be remembered**.
